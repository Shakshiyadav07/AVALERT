## 📌 Executive Summary

**AVALERT** is a next-generation AI-powered Disaster Management & Emergency Response Platform. Designed specifically for critical scenarios like avalanches, severe landslides, and flash floods, AVALERT bridges the gap between stranded citizens, field rescue teams, and relief coordinators through real-time telemetry, spatial intelligence, and automated SOS routing.

---

## 🏔️ Why AVALERT? (The Problem Statement)
Avalanches in high-altitude regions often lead to massive delays in emergency response due to lack of real-time communication and geographic isolation. AVALERT solves this by:[cite: 1]
*   **Minimizing Response Time:** Automating distress signaling to reduce human intervention latency[cite: 1].
*   **Centralizing Intelligence:** Combining map data, rescue deployment status, and relief tracking into a unified interface[cite: 1].
*   **Empowering Citizens:** Providing a simple, accessible way for individuals to request help and view live safety guidelines[cite: 1].

## 🔥 Key Modules

| Module | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| **🌐 Disaster Mapping** | Real-time geospatial visualization of high-risk zones and active rescue areas[cite: 1]. |
| **⚡ SOS Engine** | High-priority emergency trigger with location tagging and automated routing[cite: 1]. |
| **🚁 Rescue Hub** | Tactical oversight for field teams to manage deployment and rescue mission status[cite: 1]. |
| **🛡️ Citizen Portal** | Simplified interface for public distress requests and official safety updates[cite: 1]. |
| **📦 Relief & Funds** | End-to-end management for transparency in disaster aid and material distribution[cite: 1]. |
